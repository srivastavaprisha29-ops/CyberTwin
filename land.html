// THE PROBLEM

Fragments look harmless. Together, they aren't.

A single person's identity is spread across dozens of emails, usernames, profiles and forgotten accounts. Individually, each fragment seems trivial — correlated, they can expose far more than any one of them intended to.

// CORE CAPABILITIES

From detection to understanding to action.

Footprint discovery
Finds and organizes the publicly accessible fragments of an identity — usernames, emails, profiles, and exposed metadata — into one structured view.
Identity graph
Represents every discovered entity as a node, surfacing relationships between accounts, aliases and services that would stay hidden in isolation.
Exposure detection
Surfaces leaked credentials, forgotten accounts, suspicious reuse, and other exposure signals tied to the identity graph.
Digital risk score
An interpretable score built from exposure severity, reuse patterns and account criticality — explaining why the risk exists, not just what it is.
Attack-surface simulation
Walks the identity graph from an attacker's perspective, tracing plausible paths from a public username to a high-value account.
Security recommendations
Every detected risk resolves to a concrete, actionable next step — not just a flagged problem.
